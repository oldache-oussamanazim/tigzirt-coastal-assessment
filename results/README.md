# Results and observations

## 1. Shoreline reference-distance comparison at Tasalast

| Observation date as stated in report text | Distance from fixed wall reference to waterline |
|---|---:|
| November 2019 | 24.04 m |
| 2020 | 20.15 m |
| November 2023 | 17.64 m |

The difference between the first and last reported values is:

**24.04 m − 17.64 m = 6.40 m**

The report interprets the decreasing distance as local shoreline retreat toward the fixed reference point.

### Date discrepancy
The subsection heading states **November 2019 – October 2021 – November 2023**, while the explanatory paragraph states **November 2019 – 2020 – November 2023**. The middle date remains unverified and should be checked against the original Google Earth imagery.

### Interpretation limits
The 6.40 m difference is a change in distance measured from one fixed reference point to the waterline. It is not a beach-wide shoreline displacement and does not establish an annualized erosion rate. A robust rate would require verified dates, consistent shoreline indicators, measurement geometry, and an assessment of positional uncertainty and water-level effects.

## 2. Field observations
The report documents:
- Sandy beach deposits, with gravels and pebbles more frequent near stream mouths and higher-energy areas.
- Stream channels and mouths described as pathways for episodic sediment delivery.
- Sediment redistribution under wave action and coastal currents.
- Localized slope instability and landslides on slopes above parts of the beaches.
- Gabion works, a backshore retaining/protection wall, culverts at stream outlets, and port breakwaters.
- Brief resident comments concerning coastal changes, landslides, and flooding.

These points summarize the submitted report; they are not newly measured or independently validated findings.

## 3. Topographic survey data
The report describes a Topcon GM-52 total-station survey and acquisition of X, Y, Z coordinates. However, the submitted PDF does not contain a detailed coordinate table or a populated results section for the total-station survey.

The original AutoCAD files and survey exports are not currently available. Consequently, this repository does not publish a reconstructed shoreline line, topographic profile, map derived from survey coordinates, or additional numerical result.
