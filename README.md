# Coastal Geomorphological Assessment and Shoreline Evolution of Tigzirt, Algeria

## Overview
This repository documents a coastal field study conducted in April 2025 along the Tigzirt coast, wilaya of **Tizi Ouzou**, northern Algeria. Field investigations covered **Tasalast (also spelled Tasalast/Tasselast in the report)** and **Feraoun** beaches.

The objective was to characterize the coastal environment, examine ongoing coastal processes, and assess the influence of human interventions on shoreline evolution. The work combined topographic surveying, GPS positioning, direct field observations, historical-image comparison, and brief discussions with local residents.

## Methods and equipment
- **Topographic survey:** Topcon GM-52 total station used to survey characteristic shoreline points and obtain X, Y, Z coordinates.
- **Positioning:** Garmin eTrex GPS used to locate survey stations and fixed reference points.
- **Historical comparison:** Google Earth imagery inspected to compare waterline positions at Tasalast.
- **CAD:** AutoCAD used in connection with the topographic work.
- **Field observations:** beach morphology, sediment types, stream mouths, slope instability, and coastal infrastructure.
- **Local knowledge:** brief resident discussions concerning perceived coastal change, landslides, and flooding.

## Historical reference-distance comparison at Tasalast
The report uses a fixed point on the backshore protection wall (reported coordinates: 36°53'23"N, 4°06'32"E) and records the distance to the waterline:

| Date | Distance |
|---|---:|
| November 2019 | 24.04 m |
| November 2023 | 17.64 m |

The report interprets the decreasing distance as local shoreline retreat toward the fixed reference point.

**Important limitation:** this is a distance measured from one fixed reference point to the waterline, not a beach-wide shoreline displacement or a shoreline-change rate. 

## Topics covered in the field report
- Geological and tectonic context
- Hydrology and former/present stream channels
- Sediment types, transport, and distribution
- Beach and shoreline dynamics
- Localized landslides and slope instability
- Stream-mouth deposits and sediment inputs
- Coastal and backshore infrastructure, including gabions, a protection wall, and port structures
- Qualitative observations from local residents

## Repository status
This repository initially documents the submitted field report, the original AutoCAD drawings and detailed total-station coordinate exports. 
**The results will be added after recovery and verification.**

No raw survey coordinates, derived shoreline vectors, complete GIS project, or reproducible numerical shoreline-change analysis are claimed to be available at this stage.
